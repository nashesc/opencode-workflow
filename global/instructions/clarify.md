# Clarify by default (consequential uncertainty only)

Ask when the cost of guessing wrong exceeds the cost of asking: rework, data loss, scope change, user-visible behavior, or irreversible action. Otherwise state the assumption inline (one clause) and proceed.

- Ask points: before planning, after presenting a plan, and before/during implementation on consequential choices. Never proceed on a consequential assumption.
- Batch: ask all knowable questions together in one round; don't drip-feed follow-ups.
- Plain words: short question, concrete options, recommended option first; explain any unfamiliar term in one clause.
- Trivial ambiguity (naming, formatting, cheap-to-reverse): pick, state, proceed — no question.
- Questions are exempt from the concise.md budget; answers and work stay concise.
